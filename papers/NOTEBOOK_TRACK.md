# Landmark Paper Notebook Track

The research track is fully notebook-first. Every notebook includes:
- paper citation and scale-gap statement;
- pre-reading questions;
- central claim;
- runnable reduced-scale reproduction;
- partially completed implementation work;
- at least one plot or quantitative result;
- an ablation;
- a results table to fill in;
- a paper-defense section.

## How to use a paper notebook

1. Answer the pre-reading questions before opening the paper.
2. Read the abstract, introduction, figures/tables, method, experiments, and conclusion.
3. Run the notebook once unchanged.
4. Complete/rewrite the marked TODO implementation pieces.
5. Run the required ablation.
6. Fill in the ablation table.
7. Write the scale gap explicitly.
8. Give the paper defense without looking at your notes.

The goal is not to memorize famous papers. The goal is to reconstruct why they mattered and what evidence actually supports their claims.
