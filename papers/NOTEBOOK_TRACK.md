## Standard
Each notebook must contain:
- pre-reading questions;
- central claim;
- runnable reduced-scale reproduction;
- at least one plotted result or quantitative table;
- at least one ablation;
- TODO implementation work;
- explicit scale-gap statement;
- paper-defense questions.
