# Paper Activity 07: GAN

**Paper:** Goodfellow et al., "Generative Adversarial Networks" (2014), https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2661

## Reproduce
Use a two-dimensional target distribution before images.

Train generator and discriminator and animate/plot generated samples over time.

## Ablations
- discriminator much stronger than generator
- generator much stronger than discriminator
- different learning rates

## Explain
Describe the minimax game and why training can be unstable.

## Evidence
Show whether the generated distribution covers all target modes.
