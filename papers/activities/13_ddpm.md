# Paper Activity 13: DDPM

**Paper:** Ho, Jain & Abbeel, "Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models" (2020), https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239

## Reproduce
Start with a 2-D distribution.

1. implement the forward noising schedule;
2. visualize samples at multiple timesteps;
3. train a small network to predict injected noise;
4. implement iterative reverse sampling.

## Ablations
Change the noise schedule and number of diffusion steps.

## Explain
Why can learning to predict noise yield a generative model?
