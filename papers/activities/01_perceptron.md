# Paper Activity 01: The Perceptron

**Paper:** Frank Rosenblatt, "The Perceptron: A Probabilistic Model for Information Storage and Organization in the Brain" (1958), DOI: 10.1037/h0042519.

## Central question
Can a simple adaptive linear decision mechanism learn a classification boundary from examples?

## Reproduce
1. Implement the perceptron update from scratch.
2. Train on a linearly separable 2-D dataset.
3. Plot mistakes per epoch and the decision boundary.
4. Repeat on XOR.

## Landmark result to explain
Explain why convergence on separable data and failure on XOR are both informative.

## Ablation
Remove the bias term. Construct a dataset where that change matters.

## Defense
What can a single perceptron represent? What can it never represent regardless of training time?
