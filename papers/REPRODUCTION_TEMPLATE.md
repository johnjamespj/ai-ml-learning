# Paper Reproduction Report Template

## Citation
Title, authors, venue/year, stable paper link.

## 1. The world before this paper
What approach was common? What limitation motivated the work?

## 2. Central claim
One sentence. If you need a paragraph, refine it.

## 3. Mechanism
Explain the architecture/algorithm and derive the key equation(s).

## 4. What the authors measured
Dataset, split, metric, baseline, scale, and experimental assumptions.

## 5. Educational reproduction
State exactly what you are reproducing and what you are **not** reproducing.

## 6. Result
Tables/plots with seeds and configuration.

## 7. Ablation
Remove or alter one important component.

## 8. Failure analysis
Where does your reproduction fail? Is the failure from scale, implementation, data, or the idea itself?

## 9. Modern comparison
How is the idea used or modified today?

## 10. Five-minute defense
Answer:
- What problem did the paper solve?
- What was actually new?
- Which evidence supports the claim?
- What evidence is weaker than it first appears?
- Which assumption matters most?
- Would you still use this idea today, and where?
