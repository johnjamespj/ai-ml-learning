# Project 06: Mini Transformer

## Mission
Build a small Transformer language model far enough down the stack that attention is not hidden from you.

## Required components
- tokenizer or character vocabulary
- embeddings
- positional information
- your own scaled dot-product attention
- multi-head attention
- feed-forward layer
- residual connections
- normalization
- causal mask
- language-model output head
- cross-entropy training
- sampling

## Tests
- verify attention shapes
- verify causal masking
- overfit a tiny sequence
- train on a small corpus
- generate samples at several temperatures

## Report
Trace one batch through every tensor shape.
